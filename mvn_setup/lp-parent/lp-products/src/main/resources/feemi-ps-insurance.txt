================================================================================
PRODUCT SPECIFICATION: Feemi PS-vakuutus (Pitkäaikaissäästäminen)
================================================================================

PRODUCT CODE: FEEMI_PS_001
PRODUCT NAME: PS-vakuutus (Long-term Savings Insurance)
CATEGORY:     Government Incentivized Long-term Savings
TARGET:       Long-term wealth accumulation with government bonus

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Age Range:           18 - 62 years at inception
                     Must convert before age 68
Residency:           Finnish tax resident required
Employment:          Any status (income documentation for large contributions)
Health:              No medical underwriting required
Existing PS:         Cannot have active PS-vakuutus with another provider

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRIBUTION STRUCTURE  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Initial Payment:     EUR 50 - EUR 5,000
Regular Payments:    EUR 50 - EUR 416 per month (EUR 5,000 ÷ 12)
                     OR EUR 600 - EUR 5,000 per year
Lump Sum Additions:  EUR 50 minimum, subject to annual limit
Payment Methods:     Bank transfer, direct debit

Annual Limits:       EUR 5,000 maximum per year (government limit)
Min to Maintain:     EUR 50 per year (or contract converts to regular savings)
Total Lifetime Max:  EUR 50,000 (across all providers)

GOVERNMENT BONUS:
- 4.5% annually on contributions up to EUR 5,000
- Paid by Finnish government directly to account
- Calculated monthly on contribution balance
- Bonus stops at age 68 or early withdrawal

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INVESTMENT OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GUARANTEED OPTION
   - Name: "Taattu PS-tuotto"
   - Return: 1.5% guaranteed annually + 4.5% government bonus
   - Risk: None
   - Fees: 0.5% annual management fee
   
2. CONSERVATIVE PS FUND
   - Name: "Maltillinen PS-rahasto"
   - Asset Mix: 70% bonds, 30% equity
   - Expected Return: 3-4% annually + 4.5% government bonus
   - Risk: Low
   - Fees: 0.8% annual management fee
   
3. BALANCED PS FUND
   - Name: "Tasapainoinen PS-rahasto"
   - Asset Mix: 50% bonds, 50% equity
   - Expected Return: 4-6% annually + 4.5% government bonus
   - Risk: Medium
   - Fees: 1.0% annual management fee

4. GROWTH PS FUND
   - Name: "Kasvu PS-rahasto"
   - Asset Mix: 20% bonds, 80% equity
   - Expected Return: 5-8% annually + 4.5% government bonus
   - Risk: High
   - Fees: 1.2% annual management fee

ALLOCATION RULES:
- Minimum 25% per fund if multiple selected
- Maximum 100% in any single fund
- Changes allowed twice per year
- Automatic lifecycle option available

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PS-VAKUUTUS WITHDRAWAL RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum Holding:     10 years from first contribution
Early Withdrawal:    Allowed but forfeits government bonus + penalties
                     20% penalty on government bonus portion
                     Normal capital gains tax on investment growth

CONVERSION AT MATURITY (Age 68 or 10+ years):
1. FULL WITHDRAWAL
   - Account value + accumulated government bonus
   - Tax-free government bonus portion
   - Capital gains tax (30%) on investment growth only
   
2. PENSION CONVERSION
   - Convert to life annuity
   - Government bonus remains tax-free
   - Monthly payments taxed as pension income
   
3. PARTIAL WITHDRAWAL + ANNUITY
   - Take up to 50% as lump sum
   - Convert remainder to annuity
   - Government bonus pro-rated

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAX TREATMENT (Finnish Tax Code 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions:       NOT tax deductible (unlike pension insurance)
Government Bonus:    TAX-FREE (4.5% annually)
Investment Growth:   Tax-free inside contract
Withdrawals:         Capital gains tax (30%) on growth portion only
                     Government bonus portion always tax-free
Early Withdrawal:    20% penalty on government bonus + normal taxes

SPECIAL RULES:
- Government bonus earned only while Finnish tax resident
- Residency change: Can keep existing but no new bonus
- Death before conversion: Beneficiary gets full account + bonus

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEES STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup Fee:           EUR 0
Annual Policy Fee:   EUR 24 (EUR 2 per month)
Management Fee:      0.5% - 1.2% depending on investment choice
Conversion Fee:      EUR 0 (government subsidized)
Early Withdrawal:    EUR 200 + 20% penalty on government bonus

Transaction Fees:
- Regular Contribution: EUR 0
- Lump Sum Addition:    EUR 10
- Investment Switch:    EUR 15 (2 free changes per year)

Government Admin:    EUR 0 (covered by government)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS RULES & VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Maximum EUR 5,000 contributions per calendar year
2. Cannot contribute after age 68
3. Must hold minimum 10 years to get full government bonus
4. Early withdrawal forfeits government bonus + penalties
5. Only one PS-vakuutus allowed per person (across all providers)
6. Automatic conversion to pension at age 68 if no action taken
7. Government bonus calculated monthly, paid quarterly
8. Beneficiary designation required but can be changed anytime

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED DATA FIELDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POLICYHOLDER:
- Full name, Henkilötunnus
- Current age, employment status
- Annual income (for compliance reporting)
- Finnish bank account (IBAN)
- Tax advisor contact (recommended for high earners)

BENEFICIARY:
- Primary beneficiary (mandatory)
- Secondary beneficiary (optional)
- Relationship, contact information

CONTRACT:
- Contribution amount & frequency
- Investment allocation (minimum 25% per fund)
- Conversion preference at maturity
- Communication language (Finnish/Swedish)

GOVERNMENT REPORTING:
- Yearly contribution totals
- Government bonus calculations
- Tax reporting for early withdrawals
- Cross-provider verification (EUR 50,000 lifetime limit)
