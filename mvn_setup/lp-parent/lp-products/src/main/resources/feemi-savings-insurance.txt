================================================================================
PRODUCT SPECIFICATION: Feemi Säästö- ja Sijoitusvakuutus
================================================================================

PRODUCT CODE: FEEMI_SAV_001
PRODUCT NAME: Säästö- ja sijoitusvakuutus  
CATEGORY:     Investment/Savings Insurance
TARGET:       Individual wealth accumulation with tax benefits

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Age Range:           18 - 75 years at inception
                     Maximum age 85 at maturity
Residency:           Finnish tax resident required
Health:              No medical underwriting required
Min Net Worth:       No minimum requirement
Employment:          Any (employed, self-employed, unemployed, retired)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRIBUTION STRUCTURE  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Initial Payment:     EUR 1,000 - EUR 50,000
Regular Payments:    EUR 50 - EUR 5,000 per month
                     OR EUR 600 - EUR 60,000 per year
Lump Sum Additions:  EUR 500 minimum, no maximum
Payment Methods:     Bank transfer, direct debit
Payment Frequency:   Monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual

Max Annual Total:    EUR 60,000 (regulatory limit)
Min Contract Value:  EUR 1,000 (below this = forced closure)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INVESTMENT OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GUARANTEED OPTION
   - Name: "Taattu tuotto"
   - Return: 0.5% guaranteed annually  
   - Risk: None
   - Fees: 0.8% annual management fee
   
2. BALANCED FUND
   - Name: "Tasapainoinen rahasto"
   - Asset Mix: 60% bonds, 40% equity
   - Expected Return: 4-6% annually
   - Risk: Medium
   - Fees: 1.2% annual management fee
   
3. EQUITY FUND  
   - Name: "Osakerahasto"
   - Asset Mix: 90% global equity, 10% cash
   - Expected Return: 6-8% annually  
   - Risk: High
   - Fees: 1.5% annual management fee

4. INDEX FUND
   - Name: "Indeksirahasto" 
   - Asset Mix: Tracks MSCI World Index
   - Expected Return: Market return
   - Risk: High
   - Fees: 0.6% annual management fee

ALLOCATION RULES:
- Minimum 10% per fund if selected
- Maximum 100% in any single fund
- Rebalancing allowed 4 times per year
- Changes take effect next business day

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRACT TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contract Duration:   No fixed term (flexible)
Min Holding Period:  None (but tax implications if < 3 years)
Surrender:           Full surrender allowed anytime
Partial Withdrawal: Minimum EUR 500, must leave EUR 1,000 minimum

DEATH BENEFIT:
- Beneficiary receives: Account value + 1,000 EUR death benefit
- No medical questions if death benefit ≤ EUR 50,000
- Above EUR 50,000: Medical questionnaire required

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAX TREATMENT (Finnish Tax Code 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions:       NOT tax deductible
Growth:              Tax-free inside contract  
Withdrawals:         Capital gains tax (30% currently)
                     Only on gains portion, not contributions
Death Benefit:       Tax-free to beneficiaries

Special Rules:
- If held < 3 years: Full withdrawal taxed as income
- If held > 10 years: Reduced capital gains rate (consideration)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEES STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup Fee:           EUR 0 (promotional, normally EUR 50)
Annual Policy Fee:   EUR 36 (EUR 3 per month)
Management Fee:      0.6% - 1.5% depending on fund choice
Surrender Fee:       0% if held > 2 years
                     2% if surrendered in year 1
                     1% if surrendered in year 2

Transaction Fees:
- Contribution:      EUR 0
- Withdrawal:        EUR 25 per transaction
- Rebalancing:       EUR 0 (up to 4 times/year)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS RULES & VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Age + Contract Duration cannot exceed 85 years
2. Monthly contributions must be sustainable (income verification if > EUR 1,000/month)
3. Total annual contributions across all Finnish insurance savings cannot exceed EUR 60,000
4. Beneficiary designation required at inception
5. Address changes require proof of continued Finnish tax residency
6. Investment allocation must total 100%
7. Partial withdrawals cannot leave account below EUR 1,000
8. Death benefit increase > EUR 50,000 triggers underwriting

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED DATA FIELDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POLICYHOLDER:
- Full name (as per Finnish ID)
- Henkilötunnus (Finnish personal ID)
- Address (Finnish address required)
- Phone, email
- Employment status & income (if contributions > EUR 12,000/year)
- Bank account (Finnish IBAN)

BENEFICIARY:
- Full name  
- Relationship to policyholder
- Henkilötunnus or passport number
- Address

CONTRACT:
- Initial contribution amount
- Regular contribution amount & frequency  
- Investment allocation percentages
- Beneficiary designation
- Preferred communication language (Finnish/Swedish)
