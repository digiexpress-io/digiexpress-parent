================================================================================
PRODUCT SPECIFICATION: Feemi Vapaaehtoinen Eläkevakuutus
================================================================================

PRODUCT CODE: FEEMI_PEN_001
PRODUCT NAME: Vapaaehtoinen eläkevakuutus
CATEGORY:     Individual Pension Insurance
TARGET:       Retirement savings with tax benefits

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Age Range:           18 - 62 years at inception
                     Must start payments before age 68
Residency:           Finnish tax resident required
Employment:          Any status (income verification for large contributions)
Existing Pension:    Can supplement TyEL/other statutory pensions
Max Policies:        One active pension insurance per person

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRIBUTION STRUCTURE  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Initial Payment:     EUR 500 - EUR 8,500 (annual tax limit)
Regular Payments:    EUR 25 - EUR 708 per month (EUR 8,500 ÷ 12)
                     OR EUR 300 - EUR 8,500 per year
Lump Sum Additions:  EUR 100 minimum, subject to annual limit
Payment Methods:     Bank transfer, direct debit, employer deduction

ANNUAL TAX LIMITS (2024):
Max Deductible:      EUR 8,500 per year (Finnish tax code)
Employer Contrib:    Additional EUR 8,500 if employer-sponsored
Combined Max:        EUR 17,000 per year (employee + employer)

Minimum to Maintain: EUR 300 per year (or contract lapses)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INVESTMENT OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GUARANTEED PENSION
   - Name: "Taattu eläketuotto"
   - Return: 1.0% guaranteed annually
   - Risk: None
   - Fees: 0.9% annual management fee
   
2. CONSERVATIVE MIX
   - Name: "Maltillinen eläkesalkku"
   - Asset Mix: 80% bonds, 20% equity
   - Expected Return: 3-5% annually
   - Risk: Low-Medium
   - Fees: 1.1% annual management fee
   
3. BALANCED PENSION
   - Name: "Tasapainoinen eläkesalkku"
   - Asset Mix: 50% bonds, 50% equity
   - Expected Return: 4-7% annually
   - Risk: Medium
   - Fees: 1.3% annual management fee

4. GROWTH PENSION
   - Name: "Kasvueläkesalkku"
   - Asset Mix: 20% bonds, 80% equity
   - Expected Return: 5-9% annually
   - Risk: High
   - Fees: 1.4% annual management fee

LIFECYCLE OPTION:
- Automatic rebalancing based on age
- Age 18-40: 80% equity, 20% bonds
- Age 40-55: 60% equity, 40% bonds  
- Age 55-68: 40% equity, 60% bonds
- Fee: 1.2% annually

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RETIREMENT & PAYOUT OPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retirement Age:      62 - 68 years (flexible)
Mandatory Start:     Age 68 (Finnish law)

PAYOUT OPTIONS:
1. LIFE ANNUITY
   - Monthly payments for life
   - Amount based on account value + life expectancy
   - Guaranteed minimum 10 years of payments
   
2. TERM CERTAIN ANNUITY  
   - Fixed period: 5, 10, 15, or 20 years
   - Higher monthly amount than life annuity
   - Payments stop at end of term
   
3. JOINT LIFE ANNUITY
   - Payments continue for survivor
   - 50%, 75%, or 100% survivor benefit
   - Lower initial payments
   
4. PARTIAL LUMP SUM + ANNUITY
   - Take up to 25% as lump sum
   - Remaining 75% as annuity
   - Lump sum taxed as income

EARLY WITHDRAWAL:
- Before age 62: 20% penalty + income tax
- Medical emergency exception (serious illness)
- Unemployment exception (> 12 months, partial withdrawal)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TAX TREATMENT (Finnish Tax Code 2024)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions:       FULLY tax deductible (up to EUR 8,500)
Growth:              Tax-free inside contract
Retirement Payments: Taxed as INCOME (marginal tax rate)
Lump Sum:            Taxed as income in year received
Death Benefit:       Taxed as income to beneficiaries

SPECIAL RULES:
- Employer contributions: Taxable benefit unless within limits
- Early withdrawal: Income tax + 20% penalty
- Transfer between providers: Tax-free if direct transfer

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEES STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Setup Fee:           EUR 0
Annual Policy Fee:   EUR 48 (EUR 4 per month)
Management Fee:      0.9% - 1.4% depending on investment choice
Conversion Fee:      EUR 100 (when converting to annuity)
Early Withdrawal:    EUR 150 + 20% penalty

Transaction Fees:
- Regular Contribution: EUR 0
- Lump Sum Addition:    EUR 15
- Investment Switch:    EUR 25 (max 2 free per year)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS RULES & VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cannot contribute after age 68
2. Must start receiving payments by age 68
3. Annual contributions cannot exceed EUR 8,500 tax limit
4. Early withdrawal requires medical/unemployment documentation
5. Beneficiary designation required (spouse gets preference)
6. Cannot have multiple pension policies with same provider
7. Minimum 5-year contribution period before retirement
8. Investment allocation changes limited to 4 times per year

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED DATA FIELDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POLICYHOLDER:
- Full name, Henkilötunnus
- Current age, desired retirement age
- Employment status, annual income
- Existing pension arrangements (TyEL, other)
- Finnish bank account
- Tax advisor contact (if applicable)

BENEFICIARY:
- Primary and secondary beneficiaries
- Relationship, percentage split
- Contact information

CONTRACT:
- Contribution amount & frequency
- Investment allocation
- Desired retirement age
- Preferred payout method
- Employer involvement (Y/N)
