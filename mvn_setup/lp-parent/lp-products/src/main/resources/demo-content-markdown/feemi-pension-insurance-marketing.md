# Voluntary Pension Insurance

## Secure Your Future with Finland's Most Flexible Pension Plan

Planning for retirement doesn't have to be complicated or overwhelming. Our Voluntary Pension Insurance is designed for busy professionals who want a simple, tax-smart way to build wealth for their golden years.

## Key Benefits

### Tax Advantages That Work for You

- Deduct up to €8,500 annually from your taxes
- Watch your money grow completely tax-free inside your account
- If your employer contributes too, you could save up to €17,000 per year in taxes

### Investment Options for Every Comfort Level

Whether you prefer safety or growth, we have you covered:

- **Guaranteed Pension** - Sleep soundly with 1% guaranteed returns
- **Conservative Mix** - Steady growth with 80% bonds, 20% stocks
- **Balanced Pension** - Best of both worlds with equal bond and stock exposure
- **Growth Pension** - Maximum potential with 80% stocks for the ambitious
- **Lifecycle Option** - Let us adjust your investments automatically as you age

### Flexible When You Need It

- Start contributing from as little as €25 per month
- Take payment holidays when life gets busy
- Choose your retirement age between 62-68
- Multiple payout options when you retire

## Getting Started

### Simple Requirements

Getting started takes just minutes. You'll need:

- To be between 18-62 years old
- A Finnish bank account
- To decide how much you want to contribute (minimum €300 per year)

Our friendly team handles all the paperwork and keeps you informed with clear, jargon-free updates about your growing retirement fund.

## Retirement Options

### Your Money, Your Choice

When retirement arrives, you have options:

- **Monthly payments for life** - guaranteed income you can count on
- **Fixed-term payments** - higher amounts for a specific period
- **Joint coverage** - protection for your spouse
- **Partial lump sum** - take up to 25% immediately, convert the rest to monthly income
