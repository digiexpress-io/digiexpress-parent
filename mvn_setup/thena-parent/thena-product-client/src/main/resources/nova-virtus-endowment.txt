TAX TREATMENT (As of 1 January 2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INVESTMENT SWITCHING:
- Can switch investments without immediate capital gains taxation
- No need to declare investment changes on tax return

WITHDRAWAL TAXATION:
- Gains taxed when withdrawing funds or when policy expires
- Tax withheld automatically on partial withdrawals
- Net amount paid to bank account

CAPITAL/RETURN CALCULATION:
- Proportional calculation between capital and taxable return
- Example: EUR 10,000 invested → EUR 12,000 value
  - EUR 1,200 withdrawal = EUR 1,000 capital + EUR 200 taxable return

POLICY EXPIRY:
- Losses are tax-deductible (considering partial surrenders)
- Loss deductible from capital income if policy doesn't expire due to death
- Premiums for death cover included in invested amount calculation

INHERITANCE TAXATION:
- Death benefit to next-of-kin: Subject to inheritance tax in entirety
- Next-of-kin definition: Spouse, direct heirs, adopted children and their heirs, 
  foster children, spouse's children
- Non-next-of-kin beneficiaries: Capital income tax rate applied
- Estate as beneficiary: Capital income tax withheld

ESTATE PROCEDURES:
- Benefit amount and beneficiary clause declared in estate inventory
- Benefit NOT recorded under deceased's assets
- Exception: If estate is beneficiary, record under deceased's assets
- Insurance company informs Tax Administration of benefit payments
